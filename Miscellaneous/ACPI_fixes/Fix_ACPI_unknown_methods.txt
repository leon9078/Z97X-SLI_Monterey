Find: "10 4F B8 2E 5F 53 42 5F 50 43 49 30 5B 82 42 B8 58 48 43 5F"

As per ACPI spec:

DefScope := ScopeOp PkgLength NameString TermList

10 ---> ScopeOp

PkgLength can be of different length based on the most significant nybble of the 1st byte:
- "0-3" ---> no other following byte
- "4?" ---> 1 following byte
- "8?" ---> 2 following bytes
- "C?" ---> 3 following bytes

4F B8 ---> PkgLength

To encode the PkgLength value (if there're following bytes) we have to read the following bytes from right to left adding the least significant nybble of the 1st byte
In this case: B8 F ---> 0xB8F = 2959 bytes

Based on the findings inside Pattern_DSDT.txt we need to remove 152 bytes from the PkgLength
2959 - 152 = 2807

2807 = 0xAF7

We take the least significant nybble (7) and put in next to the initial most significant nybble of the 1st byte: 47
Then following byte is AF
So, we encode the new PkgLength as: "47 AF"

Same with "Device (XHC)"

DefDevice := DeviceOp PkgLength NameString TermList

5B 82 ---> DeviceOp
42 B8 ---> PkgLength

We need to remove 152 bytes from this PkgLength
0xB82 = 2946 bytes

2946 - 152 = 2794

2794 = 0xAEA

4A AE








Final Find/Replace sequence of this 1st out of 39 PkgLength value changes is:
   Find: "10 4F B8 2E 5F 53 42 5F 50 43 49 30 5B 82 42 B8 58 48 43 5F"
Replace: "10 47 AF 2E 5F 53 42 5F 50 43 49 30 5B 82 4A AE 58 48 43 5F"



We can put the same Find/Replace bytes into subexpressions for Hextor!

   Find: "(10) 4F B8 (2E 5F 53 42 5F 50 43 49 30 5B 82) 42 B8 (58 48 43 5F)"
Replace: "$1 47 AF $2 4A AE $3"
